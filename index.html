Global Branded Hospitality

Holiday Inn Express Surat Gauravpath

Developed by Yashvi Greenco LLP in partnership with IHG Hotels & Resorts. Located on Surat's primary Gauravpath arterial route, bringing international hospitality standards to business travelers visiting Surat's textile, diamond, and manufacturing corridors.

  • International standard business suites
  • Prime airport and expressway connectivity
  • High-speed connectivity & corporate amenities
Industrial Energy Logistics

Yashvi Coal & Minerals Pvt. Ltd.

Operating since 2015, specializing in the direct bulk import and distribution of premium industrial steam coal from Indonesia and South Africa. Supplying reliable energy to textile mills, power units, and manufacturing plants across Western India.

  • Direct import terminal contracts
  • Consistent thermal grade quality control
  • Established supply chain across Gujarat
Humanitarian Responsibility

Yashvi Foundation & Anand Vatika

Founded by Paresh Jhabarmal Khandelwal, Yashvi Foundation oversees meaningful social development programs in South Gujarat. Its signature initiative, Anand Vatika, provides complete housing, nutrition, healthcare, and schooling for HIV-positive orphan girls.

  • Yashvi Anand Vatika home for orphan girls
  • Regular free community medical checkup camps
  • Civic patriotism initiatives (Operation Sindoor)
